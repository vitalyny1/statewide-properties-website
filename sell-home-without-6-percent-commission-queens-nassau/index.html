Understand the Difference Between Listing and Buyer-Agent Compensation

A listing commission pays for the listing services described in your agreement. Any buyer-agent compensation is optional, separately negotiable, and must be agreed to in writing. If agreed, it may increase your total transaction costs.

Compare Your Total Selling Costs

When comparing proposals, review the listing fee, service scope, any optional buyer-agent compensation, and other potential transaction expenses. Do not assume that a headline percentage includes every cost.

Get the Listing Terms in Writing
