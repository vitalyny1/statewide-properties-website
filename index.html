What If We Bring You the Buyer?

If we bring the buyer directly, there is no separate buyer-agent compensation expense because there is no buyer's agent involved in that transaction.

What If the Buyer Has Their Own Agent?

If a buyer comes with their own agent and presents a great offer, we can discuss whether offering compensation to that agent makes sense for your sale. You will have an opportunity to consider the offer and the proposed terms before deciding whether to agree to any buyer-agent compensation.

Every offer is different. Our goal is to help you evaluate your options and make an informed decision that works for you.

The 1% total listing commission applies to the listing services specified in your written listing agreement. Any buyer-agent compensation, if offered and agreed to, is a separate negotiable arrangement and may increase the seller’s total transaction costs.
