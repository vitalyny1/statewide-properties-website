Frequently Asked Questions

Here are a few things to know before getting started.

What does the 1% commission cover?

The proposed 1% is the listing-side commission. The specific services included, any additional costs, and any exclusions will be explained in your written listing agreement.

Does the 1% include compensation for the buyer's agent?

No. Any buyer-agent compensation is separate and negotiable. It is not automatically included in the listing commission. Any such arrangement should be discussed and documented separately.

Which areas do you serve?

Our primary service area is Queens and Nassau County, New York. Contact us to confirm availability for your specific property.

Am I committed when I submit the contact form?

No. Sending an inquiry is simply a way to start a conversation. A listing relationship is established only through the appropriate written agreement.

What happens after I contact you?

We can discuss your property, your goals, the proposed services, and the commission terms. You can review the agreement before deciding whether to proceed.

Are all real estate expenses included?

Not necessarily. Services, third-party expenses, marketing costs, and other terms depend on the written agreement. Please confirm the details before signing.
