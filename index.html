How does the 1% total listing commission work?

The 1% total listing commission is for the listing services specified in your written agreement. Any buyer-agent compensation is optional, separately negotiable, and only applies if you agree to it in writing. If you agree to buyer-agent compensation, your total selling costs may be higher than 1%.

What happens if you bring the buyer directly?

If we bring the buyer directly and the buyer is not represented by a buyer's agent, there is no separate buyer-agent compensation expense. The listing commission and services remain governed by your written listing agreement.

What if the buyer has their own agent?

If a buyer comes with their own agent and presents a strong offer, we can discuss whether offering compensation to that agent makes sense. Any buyer-agent compensation is negotiable and would need to be agreed to in writing.
