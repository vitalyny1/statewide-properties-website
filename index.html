Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our listing service.

What does your 1% listing commission include?

The proposed listing commission covers the services specified in your written listing agreement, which may include MLS marketing, showing coordination, offer review, negotiation, and transaction coordination. Confirm the exact scope and exclusions before signing.

Is this a full-service listing?

Yes. The service is designed to provide listing representation and guidance throughout the sale. The exact services will be confirmed in the listing agreement.

Do I have to pay the buyer's agent?

Buyer-agent compensation is negotiable and is not automatically included in our listing commission. Any offer of compensation must be separately agreed to in writing.

What if the buyer comes directly through you?

If a buyer comes directly through our brokerage and no buyer-agent compensation is owed under a separate agreement, no additional buyer-agent compensation would be due for that buyer. The applicable agreements and circumstances control.

Do you coordinate showings and open houses?

We help coordinate showing requests and discuss open-house arrangements. The exact arrangements and any additional costs will be confirmed with you.

Which areas do you serve?

Our target service area is Queens and Nassau County, New York. Expand the Service Areas section below and contact us to confirm availability for your address.

How do I get started?

Send a consultation request with your name, property address, and contact information. We can then discuss your goals, timing, commission, and listing agreement.

Are there other costs involved in selling?

Other costs may include attorney fees, transfer taxes, mortgage-related charges, inspections, or optional marketing services. These are not necessarily included in the listing commission.
