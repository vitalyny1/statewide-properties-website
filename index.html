Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our full-service 1% listing option.

What does your 1% listing commission include?

The proposed 1% listing commission covers the services described in your written listing agreement, which may include MLS listing and marketing, showing coordination, offer review, negotiation, and transaction coordination through closing. Confirm the exact scope and any exclusions before signing.

Is this a full-service real estate listing?

Yes. The service is designed to provide professional listing representation and guidance through the sale, rather than simply placing a property on the MLS. The specific services will be confirmed in the listing agreement.

Do I have to pay the buyer's agent?

Buyer-agent compensation is negotiable and is not automatically included in our listing commission. You may choose whether to offer compensation to a buyer's agent, subject to a separate written agreement. Any such amount should be discussed before you commit to it.

What if the buyer contacts us directly?

If the buyer comes directly through our brokerage and no buyer-agent compensation is owed under a separate agreement, there would be no additional buyer-agent compensation for that buyer. The details depend on the transaction and the agreements in place.

Do you handle showings and open houses?

We help coordinate showing requests and discuss open-house arrangements. The exact arrangements, including who will attend and any associated costs, will be confirmed with you.

Which areas do you serve?

Our target service area is Queens and Nassau County, New York. Expand the Service Areas section below to see the neighborhoods and communities we aim to serve. Contact us to confirm service availability for a particular property.

How do I get started?

Send a consultation request with your name, property address, and contact information. We can then discuss your property, timing, the proposed commission, and the listing agreement.

Are there other costs involved in selling?

There may be other costs, such as attorney fees, transfer taxes, mortgage-related charges, inspections, or optional marketing services. These are not necessarily included in the listing commission. Ask for an explanation of anticipated costs before entering into an agreement.
