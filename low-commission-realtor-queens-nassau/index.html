An Affordable Alternative to a Traditional Listing Fee

If you have searched for an affordable realtor, a discount real estate agent, or a low-commission listing broker near you, you are likely comparing the cost of selling with the level of service included. The important question is not only the commission percentage; it is also what the listing professional will do to help market the property and guide the transaction.

Our approach is to discuss your property, your goals, and the services you need before you decide. The agreed scope and listing commission are documented in a written listing agreement. There is no obligation to list simply by contacting us.

What Does the 1% Listing Commission Mean?

The 1% is the listing commission for the listing services specified in the applicable written listing agreement. It is not a promise that every possible seller cost is included.

  • The listing services and fee are discussed and agreed in writing.
  • All real estate commissions are negotiable.
  • Any buyer-agent compensation is optional, separately negotiable, and must be agreed to in writing.
  • If buyer-agent compensation is agreed, it may increase the seller's total transaction costs.

We will explain the proposed service scope and terms so you can make an informed decision before signing a listing agreement.

Questions Sellers Often Ask

Is a low-commission realtor the same as a limited-service broker?

Not necessarily. Service levels vary by brokerage and agreement. Ask what marketing, showing coordination, offer guidance, and transaction support are included, and make sure the agreed scope is in writing.

Do you serve sellers outside Queens and Nassau County?

Full-service 1% listing representation through Statewide Properties LLC is focused on Queens and Nassau County. For flat-fee MLS listing options elsewhere in New York State, visit FlatFeeMLSNY.com; that is a separate service.

Does the 1% listing commission include buyer-agent compensation?

No buyer-agent compensation is automatically included. It is optional, separately negotiable, and must be agreed to in writing. If agreed, it may increase the seller's total transaction costs.

How do I compare affordable real estate agents?

Compare the written service scope, listing marketing plan, communication, showing arrangements, offer support, transaction guidance, and all fees—not just the headline commission rate.
