What the 1% Listing Commission Covers

The 1% is the listing commission for the listing services specified in the applicable written listing agreement. The agreement explains the services and fee for your listing; it is not a blanket promise that every possible seller expense is included.

Buyer-Agent Compensation Is a Separate Decision

Any buyer-agent compensation is optional, separately negotiable, and must be agreed to in writing. It is not automatically included in the 1% listing commission. If agreed, it may increase the seller’s total transaction costs.
